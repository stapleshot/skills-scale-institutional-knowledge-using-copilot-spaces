# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality Assurance / Testing Specialist

### Role Summary
QA specialists ensure features meet quality standards and acceptance criteria before release. They collaborate with developers, product managers, and operations to validate functionality, identify defects, and improve test coverage.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain automated and manual test cases
- Execute test plans and create defect reports
- Validate acceptance criteria are met before marking work as done
- Collaborate on test strategy and coverage planning
- Identify and escalate quality risks
- Contribute to Definition of Done standards

### Goals
- Ensure products are reliable and meet user expectations
- Reduce production defects through comprehensive testing
- Enable rapid, confident deployments

### Typical Communication
- Participation in sprint planning and backlog refinement
- Test case reviews and defect triage discussions
- Pre-release quality signoff gates

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works closely with **Developers** to understand implementation details and coordinate test coverage
- Collaborates with **Product Managers** to clarify acceptance criteria and validate solutions
- Partners with **Operations/DevOps** on production validation and rollback scenarios
- Supports **Project Managers** with quality metrics and release readiness assessments

---

## Design / UX Lead

### Role Summary
Design leads define the user experience and visual direction for features. They collaborate with product managers and developers to ensure solutions are usable, accessible, and aligned with brand standards.

### Responsibilities
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Define accessibility and design system standards
- Collaborate on feature scoping and trade-offs
- Review implemented designs against specifications
- Advocate for user needs and usability

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences
- Maintain design consistency across features
- Reduce rework through early validation

### Typical Communication
- Design critique sessions and feedback
- Weekly design and product alignment
- User research findings and recommendations

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partners with **Product Managers** to translate customer needs into design solutions
- Collaborates with **Developers** during implementation to ensure design fidelity
- Supports **Project Managers** in scoping and timeline planning for design work
- Works with **QA/Testing** to define usability and accessibility test criteria

---

## Engineering Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Engineering leads provide technical direction, mentorship, and architecture oversight for complex features and systems. They ensure technical quality, scalability, and alignment with long-term architectural goals.

### Responsibilities
- Review and approve technical design and architecture decisions
- Mentor developers and support skill growth
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Ensure adherence to code standards and best practices
- Facilitate technical design reviews
- Contribute to long-term technology roadmap

### Goals
- Maintain high code quality and system reliability
- Enable faster, more confident feature delivery
- Reduce technical debt and improve system scalability

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and code review guidance
- Architecture discussion with cross-functional teams
- Technical mentoring and knowledge sharing

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Mentors **Developers** and reviews technical implementations
- Collaborates with **Product Managers** on feasibility and technical trade-offs
- Works with **Project Managers** on technical risk assessment and timeline planning
- Partners with **Operations/DevOps** on scalability, reliability, and deployment strategies
- Engages **Stakeholders/Business Sponsors** to explain technical decisions and long-term architectural investments

---

## Operations / DevOps

### Role Summary
Operations teams manage infrastructure, deployment pipelines, monitoring, and production support. They ensure systems are reliable, secure, and ready for release.

### Responsibilities
- Manage CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation
- Monitor production systems and respond to incidents
- Define and enforce security and compliance standards
- Manage infrastructure and scaling
- Provide runbooks and operational documentation
- Collaborate on release planning and rollback procedures

### Goals
- Ensure high system availability and performance
- Enable rapid, safe deployments
- Minimize production incidents and recovery time

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and deployment planning
- Incident response and post-mortems
- Infrastructure and security requirements gathering

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Supports **Developers** with deployment automation and production troubleshooting
- Collaborates with **QA/Testing** on smoke tests and production validation
- Partners with **Project Managers** on release planning and deployment scheduling
- Works with **Engineering Leads** on architecture, scalability, and reliability concerns
- Reports to **Stakeholders/Business Sponsors** on system health and incident impact

---

## Stakeholder / Business Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and business sponsors provide business context, budget, and prioritization guidance. They represent customer needs and business objectives to the delivery team.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success metrics
- Provide ongoing feedback and validation
- Approve scope, budget, and timeline trade-offs
- Remove blockers and escalate resource constraints
- Communicate project status to leadership and customers
- Validate delivered outcomes against business goals

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value and ROI
- Maintain alignment between delivery and business priorities
- Reduce scope creep and misalignment

### Typical Communication
- Milestone reviews and status updates
- Decision gates and approval checkpoints
- Ad-hoc escalation and issue resolution

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Provides direction and constraints to **Project Managers** and **Product Managers**
- Reviews outcomes and measures success with **Product Managers**
- Approves major technical decisions with **Engineering Leads**
- Communicates status and risks from **Project Managers** and delivery teams
- Validates delivered features and business impact with the full team

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Understanding cross-role interactions helps team members anticipate dependencies and communication needs during project execution.
